About the plotted solar panels

Blue rectangles represent proposed modules; green outlines represent candidate roofs. The planner prioritizes average energy per panel, while the roof ranking compares total potential energy. Layouts use average roof sunlight and do not detect existing panels, obstructions, slope, or required setbacks.

Next steps

Roof addresses are suggested Google reverse-geocoding matches. Nearby matches are marked approximate. Looking up addresses makes additional Geocoding API requests.

What these results mean

Detection uses Google’s rooftop mask and connected regions. Touching roofs may merge. These are screening estimates using horizontal footprint area; they don’t assess roof condition, slope, setbacks, or installation readiness. Missing sunlight data is excluded from the energy estimate. A “partial” flag means the roof reaches the search boundary.
