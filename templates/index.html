        Sources

        • Installed cost: NREL Q1 2024 U.S. residential PV benchmark, $3.15/W (2023 dollars), with illustrative regional multipliers and a $0.35/W module allowance (modeling assumption). NREL report ↗
        • Sunlight, roofs and imagery: Google Solar API data layers. System efficiency 0.85 (inverter, wiring and soiling losses), matching Google's default.
        • Electricity prices: EIA residential average price by state, 2025 (preliminary). EIA ↗
        • CO₂: EPA eGRID2023 U.S. non-baseload rate, 1,372 lb/MWh. EPA ↗ · Average home: EIA, about 10,400 kWh/year.
        • Panels: manufacturer datasheets and retailer prices in data/panel_catalog.csv (checked 2026-10-10/11).
        • Existing-panel detection: color check; on 48 hand-labeled roofs from 7 sites it was right 73% of the time (68% precision, 65% recall). Labels were made by eye, not verified.

        Data & limitations

        Detection uses Google’s rooftop mask and connected regions. Touching roofs may merge. These are screening estimates using horizontal footprint area; they don’t assess roof condition, slope, setbacks, or installation readiness. Missing sunlight data is excluded from the energy estimate. A “partial” flag means the roof reaches the search boundary. Imagery date and attribution appear beneath the map.
