    Why these panel locations?

    Panel totals and roof-by-roof reasoning

    Blue rectangles represent proposed modules; green outlines represent candidate roofs. The planner prioritizes average energy per panel, while the roof ranking compares total potential energy. Orange areas are possible existing panels flagged by color (not a trained model); new panels avoid them. Layouts use average roof sunlight and do not detect other obstructions, slope, or required setbacks.

    Search findings

    Next steps

    Roof addresses are suggested Google reverse-geocoding matches. Nearby matches are marked approximate. Looking up addresses makes additional Geocoding API requests.

    Data & limitations

    Detection uses Google’s rooftop mask and connected regions. Touching roofs may merge. These are screening estimates using horizontal footprint area; they don’t assess roof condition, slope, setbacks, or installation readiness. Missing sunlight data is excluded from the energy estimate. A “partial” flag means the roof reaches the search boundary. Imagery date and attribution appear beneath the map.
